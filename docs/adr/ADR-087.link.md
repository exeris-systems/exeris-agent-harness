# ADR-087 — `exeris-bot`, the CI publication and capture step (link stub)

**Authoritative copy:** [`exeris-docs/adr/ADR-087-establish-exeris-bot-as-the-ci-publication-and-capture-step.md`](https://github.com/exeris-systems/exeris-docs/blob/main/adr/ADR-087-establish-exeris-bot-as-the-ci-publication-and-capture-step.md)

ADR-087 gives the organisation identities to act under and says how each is registered, what it may
reach, and how its key is kept (§A.1–A.2, Engineering Protocol 1).

## Why this stub exists

The identity this repository mints tokens for is the third of those, and it is registered the same
way as the other two with two differences worth finding from here rather than from the ADR: its key
must live on a maintainer's machine, because the harness mints locally rather than in a workflow,
and its rotation baseline is the date it was registered.

§A.1 also draws the line this repository is on one side of: the identity here writes code and opens
pull requests, and the identities in ADR-087 publish verdicts and rows. Neither does the other's
work, which is why this App is never installed on the repositories that hold records.

A reader who finds a commit authored by `exeris-agent[bot]`, or a draft pull request opened by it,
and asks what decided that it exists and what bounds it, is asking about ADR-087 §A and about
[`policy/README.md`](../../policy/README.md).
